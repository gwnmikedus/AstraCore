# Pilot beta and public-release readiness

This is a protocol for [#113](https://github.com/gwnmikedus/AstraCore/issues/113), not a completed beta report or approval to open access. The maintainer decides when to recruit, whom to admit, and whether to change the invite gate. This document changes no access rules, EVE permissions, telemetry or deployment configuration.

English is the product standard. Dutch translation [#82](https://github.com/gwnmikedus/AstraCore/issues/82) is closed as not planned; continue improving plain-English copy and glossary help. Test whether participants understand the words and assumptions instead of treating a glossary link as proof of understanding.

Use the [session and report template](templates/pilot-beta-session.md) for each session. The [fictional tour](demo/README.md) can help prepare a moderator, but its fixed screenshot fixtures do not demonstrate successful planning, profitability or an unaided first session.

## Evidence and decisions

Keep three kinds of evidence separate:

- **Engineering:** a named commit, isolated synthetic scenario, test result and browser/device actually checked. Passing tests proves the tested behavior, not a pilot's understanding or a live selling route.
- **Pilot sessions:** observations from 5–8 consenting English-speaking PI pilots across several communities, including experienced pilots and people starting or returning to PI. Include both one-character and multiple-character use. Record an alias and broad experience category; a public report does not need character names or affiliations.
- **Operational readiness:** the deployed build, maintainer review of correctness/trust, monitored availability, backup retrieval/restore, contact ownership and supported geography. A local script or simulated outage cannot establish an off-site service is working.

The matrix below is a collection plan. **Every readiness decision starts as Not assessed.** A reviewer must record build, date, evidence, unresolved failures and an owner before changing it. Earlier screenshots or CI results should not silently become evidence for a newer deployment. Record Ready, Blocked or Needs follow-up with a reason; no checkbox below automatically authorizes release.

A proposed change or passing PR check is **pending delivery** until merged and deployed. Match evidence to the actual tested commit and deployment; an open PR is not a shipped capability. In particular, validate Area/selling coverage [PR #185](https://github.com/gwnmikedus/AstraCore/pull/185), P4 feasibility [PR #184](https://github.com/gwnmikedus/AstraCore/pull/184), scan behavior [PR #181](https://github.com/gwnmikedus/AstraCore/pull/181) and backup support [PR #187](https://github.com/gwnmikedus/AstraCore/pull/187) only when the tested build includes them. Check their current status rather than carrying a readiness conclusion from this document.

## Session protocol

1. **Prepare and consent.** The maintainer selects the tested build and confirms access and a contact for problems. Explain that participation is voluntary, what notes will be kept, where, and when they will be deleted. Recording or sharing screenshots requires the participant's permission. The maintainer controls recruitment; this document sends no invitations.
2. **Give an outcome, not click instructions.** Ask: “Plan a PI operation that fits your routine, then show what you would do next and how you would sell its output.” Let the pilot choose their routine. Do not promise higher earnings or less work.
3. **Observe the first attempt.** Let the pilot read access/scopes, link characters, complete the guided path and review a saved result. Ask them to explain the plan, next upkeep action and selling route in their own words. Record points of hesitation or misunderstanding. Do not require an in-game purchase, contract or colony change to complete a session.
4. **Record assistance honestly.** Separate independent completion from completion with hints or direct intervention. If they are stuck, record the blocker before helping. A fixed screenshot demo, expert moderator instructions or an already-completed setup is not an unaided first-session success.
5. **Try a relevant contrast.** Use an isolated fictional case for a missing skill, excluded character, unavailable geography, shallow market, stale data or failed request. Never alter another pilot's profile or use production data to fabricate an error. Do not ask a participant to unlink a needed character or reset their real plan as a test.
6. **Debrief.** Ask which assumption they trusted least, what they expected a control to do, and whether they can find help/contact. Collect one concrete improvement before discussing proposed fixes.
7. **Triage and close the session.** File reproducible UX/correctness issues linked to #53/#113, with one type/status label and a fictional reproduction. Report security concerns privately under [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md). Keep sensitive source notes out of public issues and pull requests.

## Privacy-safe reporting

Use session aliases such as B01 and broad experience/device categories. Record task outcomes, assistance and aggregate counts rather than character IDs, colony locations, wallet/inventory values or affiliations. Never capture tokens, session cookies, EVE login URLs, authorization state, private security reports, `.env`, databases or unredacted logs.

Before sharing a screenshot, remove identifying names, portraits and locations, inspect the URL and surrounding UI, and retain only what explains the problem. Prefer reproducing it in a fresh fictional local profile. If it cannot be reproduced safely in public, give the maintainer a private report through the project's security/contact process. Feedback submission itself does not authorize publishing the pilot's identity.

The maintainer sets storage access and a deletion date for notes and any consented recordings. Public issue evidence should stand on a privacy-safe reproduction, not a private-session link other contributors cannot access. Do not enable usage measurement from [#112](https://github.com/gwnmikedus/AstraCore/issues/112) as part of this protocol.

## Aggregate review and public-release decision

Summarize the actual participant count, experience/community breadth, one-/multi-character coverage, devices, independently completed tasks, assisted completions and unresolved blockers. Keep qualitative confusion reports alongside the counts; five scripted completions do not establish a clear interface. If any participant cannot complete the core path unaided, keep the first-session gate blocked, fix or explain the blocker, and repeat the affected task with a participant who has not learned the solution from the moderator.

For each matrix row, record evidence and a responsible reviewer. The maintainer records the intended public build, known limits, rollback/contact arrangement and decision. Unresolved trust or data-loss failures block release. Geography or usability gaps require an explicit decision about supported scope and visible limits; do not hide them in a green test summary.

[Community Tools listing #74](https://github.com/gwnmikedus/AstraCore/issues/74), changing the invite gate, publishing a beta conclusion and deploying a public build remain separate maintainer actions. #113 stays open until the real sessions and readiness review are completed; merging these documents closes only the focused documentation issue.
