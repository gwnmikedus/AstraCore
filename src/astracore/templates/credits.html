Tools, data & community

AstraCore is its own small fan-made product, but it stands entirely on other people's work. Thank you all.

EVE Online

  • Fenris Creations (formerly CCP Games) for EVE Online, the ESI API and EVE SSO that every character, colony, skill, contract and price comes from, and the EVE Image Server for item, planet and structure images, portraits and logos.
  • EVE Online material is used under the EVE Developer License: © 2014 CCP hf. All rights reserved. "EVE", "EVE Online", "CCP", and all related logos and images are trademarks or registered trademarks of CCP hf. AstraCore is not made, endorsed or supported by Fenris Creations.

EVE community

  • DalShooth/EVE_PI_Templates: every PI template AstraCore PI suggests.
  • Fuzzwork (Steve Ronuken): the static data export with PI schematics.
  • VOC Hauling (United East-Indian Compagnie): courier tariff and buyback.
  • Dutch Legions and Insidious.: the corporation and alliance AstraCore grew up in.

Open source

  • Lucide icons (ISC), and the Barlow and IBM Plex Mono typefaces (SIL Open Font License).
  • FastAPI, Starlette, Uvicorn, Jinja, HTTPX, Pydantic.
  • PuLP and the COIN-OR CBC solver: the planner's maths.
  • cryptography and PyJWT: encrypted tokens and EVE login checks.
  • Python and SQLite.

Hosting

  • Cloudflare Tunnel puts a Mac on the internet without opening ports.
