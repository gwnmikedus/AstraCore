Before you undock

A few things worth knowing.

Can I use this if I haven’t started PI?

Yes. Your characters’ skills determine the colony slots and templates they can use. Choose your area and routine, then follow the build list in your plan. You place the colonies in EVE yourself.

Does AstraCore control my characters?

Read-only by default. AstraCore reads skills, colonies, hangars, contracts, orders, and wallet data. Sending waypoints to EVE is a separate opt-in for each character. See every permission in plain words.

How reliable are the income numbers?

They are forecasts, not guaranteed income. The planner accounts for customs tax, courier fees, and selling taxes. Planet richness starts as an estimate; your extractor data can calibrate it. The plan labels estimated yield and shows how a lower or higher yield affects income.
