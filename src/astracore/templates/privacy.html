Privacy

What is stored

  • Your profile: a display name, which character is your main, your module settings and saved plans.
  • For each linked character: its EVE character ID and name, corporation and alliance, and the EVE data listed on What we read (colonies, skills, PI products in hangars, contracts, sell orders, wallet entries related to PI, standings).
  • EVE login tokens, encrypted.
  • For each signed-in browser: when it signed in and was last seen, its user agent and the first half of its IP address.

Where

On a single machine run by the person who operates this site (it is online only while that machine runs it), in a database that only this site reads. Nothing is sent anywhere else except the requests to EVE's own servers (ESI and EVE SSO) and the public VOC Hauling site.

What we don't do

  • No tracking, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts.
  • No selling or sharing of data.
  • One cookie, ac_session, to keep you signed in. A theme choice is kept in your browser.

How to delete it

  • One character: Profile → Remove. Its EVE login is revoked and its data deleted.
  • Only a module's data: Profile → Danger zone → Reset {% for m in modules %}{{ m.name }}{% endfor %}.
  • Everything: Profile → Danger zone → Reset profile (download your data first if you want it).
  • You can also revoke AstraCore on EVE's third-party applications page (account management on eveonline.com).
