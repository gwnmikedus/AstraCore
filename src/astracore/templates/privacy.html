AstraCore / Your data

Privacy

What is stored

  • Your profile: a display name, which character is your main, your module settings and saved plans.
  • For each linked character: its EVE character ID and name, corporation and alliance, and the EVE data listed on What we read (colonies, skills, PI products in hangars, contracts, sell orders, wallet entries related to PI, standings).
  • EVE login tokens, encrypted.
  • For each signed-in browser: when it signed in and was last seen, its user agent and the first half of its IP address.
  • If you submit feedback: the text you confirmed, its submission time and delivery status, linked privately to your profile.

Where

On a single machine run by the person who operates this site (it is online only while that machine runs it), in a database that only this site reads. EVE data is requested from EVE's servers (ESI and EVE SSO), and courier tariffs from the public VOC Hauling site. Community issue status is read from GitHub.

Public feedback on GitHub

When you submit an idea or bug report, you first preview the content and explicitly confirm public publication. The confirmed title, report text and app version are sent to AstraCore's public GitHub issue tracker. Anyone can read or copy them. We do not automatically include your character identity, EVE tokens, wallets, assets, colony locations, plans or logs.

Queued reports remain private to you and the administrator until GitHub confirms publication. You can withdraw a report while it is still queued. A report whose delivery is unconfirmed may already exist on GitHub; contact the maintainer before removal.

What we don't do

  • No tracking, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts.
  • No selling of data. Public feedback is shared only after your explicit confirmation.
  • One cookie, ac_session, to keep you signed in. Your theme choice and preference for showing technical release details are kept in your browser.

How to delete it

  • One character: Profile → Remove. Its EVE login is revoked and its data deleted.
  • Only a module's data: Profile → Danger zone → Reset {% for m in modules %}{{ m.name }}{% endfor %}.
  • Everything: Profile → Danger zone → Reset profile (download your data first if you want it).
  • You can also revoke AstraCore on EVE's third-party applications page (account management on eveonline.com).
  • Resetting your profile deletes your private feedback ownership and queued reports here, but does not remove an issue already published on GitHub. Contact the maintainer about public issue content; public copies may persist elsewhere.
