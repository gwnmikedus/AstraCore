What we read from your characters

Signing in with EVE asks for no permissions at all: it only tells us which character you are. Each module asks for a set of read permissions it needs to work ("required"), and you can switch on extras per character ("optional"). You see EVE's own list of permissions every time you log in.

We never write to your account, except: setting waypoints if you enable "Send routes to your EVE client".

Everything else

Universe data (systems, planets, sovereignty), market prices and the VOC Hauling tariff come from public sources and are shared by everyone. Tokens are stored encrypted. See Privacy for what is stored and how to delete it.

You can revoke AstraCore at any time on EVE's third-party applications page (account management on eveonline.com), or remove a character on your profile page, which revokes the login for you.
